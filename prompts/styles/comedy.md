Comedy: the joke is the priority. Rebuild punchlines, puns and wordplay so
they are funny in the target language, even when that means replacing the
literal content with a local equivalent of the same kind. Keep timing: the
punchline stays in the cue where it lands. Use colloquial, idiomatic
language and the target culture's comedic register (sarcasm, irony,
understatement) to match the source.
